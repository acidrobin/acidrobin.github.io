Jonathan Clayton

I am an expert in Natural Language Processing, currently completing a PhD at the University of Sheffield. I have successfully defended my thesis on “Graphical Summarisation of Argumentative Text”, and I will be available to work from January 2025.

The core of my PhD research has involved fine-tuning Large Language Models (LLMs) for Argument Mining tasks, using PyTorch. Alongside this, I designed a novel task within my subfield of NLP, named Argument Summary Graph Parsing. I have created and annotated multiple corpora to support this task.

I am the first author of three peer-reviewed papers published in leading NLP and speech processing venues, including COMMA (Computational Models of Argument), the 2023 Argument Mining workshop at COLING, and Interspeech.

Prior to studying at Sheffield, I studied the MSc program in Speech and Language Processing at the University of Edinburgh. I have completed two internships; one at SpeakUnique, researching the application of Machine Learning to Silent Speech Detection; and the other at the Digital Humanities Institute at Sheffield University, in which I created a pipeline for the processing of historical texts using PyTorch, as part of the Linguistic DNA project.
